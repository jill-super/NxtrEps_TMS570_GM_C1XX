## Scope

`utp/` folders contain Tessy unit-test artefacts: contracts (Vector-generated RTE stubs),
backups (`.tmb`), compiled databases (`.pdb`) and **report PDFs/CHMs**.
There are 66 report PDFs and 149 compiled-help reports in this snapshot.

Report PDFs are machine-generated test logs, not design documentation, so they are
**catalogued here rather than converted page-by-page**. Open them directly in the repository.
